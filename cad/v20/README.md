# V20 Falcon Jet CAD (rev C)

Generated by `tools/v20/geometry.py` (`make v20`); do not edit the files by hand - change `tools/v20/v20params.py` and
regenerate. Write-up: [docs/V20_FALCON_JET.md](../../docs/V20_FALCON_JET.md). Rev C uses one outer mould line for both
scales: the mini is an exact 1:10 model of the full-scale aircraft.

## `mini/` - the 1:10 printable aircraft

- `v20_mini_print_set.3mf`: every printed piece in one unsliced file (millimetres), including the display / balance /
  charging stand (`stand_*`). All pieces fit the Bambu Lab P1S bed; the larger parts were cut with flat joints, and every
  piece is a closed (watertight) solid.
- `stl/`: the same pieces one by one.
- `foam/v20_mini_foam_templates.svg`: the 1:1 ruddervator template (3 mm Depron, cut two, flip one) with the pivot-rod
  line. Print at 100 % and check the 50 mm scale bar.
- `parts.json`: every part of the mini (printed, bought, envelopes) with its layer, size and centroid.
- `checks.json`: the generated checks - insides within the body (servos within the wing or spine), no internal
  collisions, the measured prop tip gap, watertight solids, P1S fit.

Print the airframe (fuselage, spine, body and wing pieces, intakes, elevons, louvres, bay doors) in LW-PLA, foamed, one
wall; ducts, nozzle and stand in PETG; the canopy is the buck for a 0.5 mm vacuum-formed PETG shell, which is also the
battery hatch (magnets). The intake ducts and thrust tube are rolled from PET film. The tube holes are already in the
pieces: the 6 mm main spar (two halves) and the 4 mm centre spar through the strip between the front and rear ducts.
Slide the carbon tubes through while gluing the joints so the pieces line up. The top louvres hinge on 0.8 mm wire and
close with a thin elastic; the bottom louvres are linked to one servo per side. The payload bay doors hinge on their
outer edges and open sideways.

## `full/` - the full-scale concept

Outer-mould-line STLs in millimetres for visualisation and further design work. They are **not** manufacturing data:
the full-scale aircraft is a concept study, not a build package.
